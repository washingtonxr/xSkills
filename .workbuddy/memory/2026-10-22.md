- Wi-Fi 6: RU 26/52/106/242/484/996/2x996; SCS 312.5→78.125 kHz (4x), symbol 12.8 µs, GI 0.8/1.6/3.2 µs; 1024-QAM MCS10/11 (+25%); BSS Color 6-bit in SIG-A; dual NAV; OBSS_PD -82..-62 dBm coupled to TX power; TF+BSRP+UORA; TWT; 9.6 Gbps. - Wi-Fi 7: 320 MHz, 4096-QAM MCS12/13, 8 SS (16 dropped), MRU, puncturing, 23 Gbps. MLO upper/lower MAC split; modes MLSR/EMLSR/MLMR-STR/MLMR-NSTR/EMLMR; TTLM, SCS, R-TWT.
- Wi-Fi 9: WNG SC contributions early 2026, PAR/CSD ~Jan 2027, commercial 2030s. Reference: Karamyshev, Levitsky, Khorov, "Towards Wi-Fi 9", Problems of Information Transmission vol 61 pp 383-406, DOI 10.1134/S0032946025040040. Priorities = determinism/reliability/new domains, NOT nominal rate. Drivers: AI inverting uplink asymmetry, robotics sub-ms latency, immersive. AI architecture = LAN controller + APs, CSI/I-Q collection, digital-twin synthetic data, model distribution w/ versioning+rollback, federated learning, greenfield legacy-decoupled modes.
- Useful quantitative caveat: a UL-OFDMA round costs ~500-800 µs regardless of payload; 26-tone RU ≈14.7 Mbps vs full 242-tone ≈143 Mbps (OFDMA trades per-client rate for aggregate efficiency).
