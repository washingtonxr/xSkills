## WLAN — new note 02-16, radio resource management

### What was written
- `knowledge-graph/WLAN/02-16 WLAN - Radio Resource Management - Channels, Power, Admission and the
  Roaming Algorithms.md` — 1286 lines, 15 Mermaid diagrams, 27 tables, 30 references, English only.
- Thesis: IEEE 802.11 defines measurements (11k), requests (11v), fast security (11r) and etiquette
  (11h) but never says who assigns a channel or when a STA must move. RRM is a vendor function built
  on standardised primitives, and every argument reduces to: what can it measure, what may it force,
  what will the client do anyway.
- Sections 0-14: what RRM manages (spectrum / power / airtime / association) and the cellular contrast;
  the loop plus the authority-gradient table (six of ten actuators are refusable); the measurement
  catalogue and its cost; channel assignment as a weighted graph problem with the Cisco Cost Metric;
  five algorithm families positioned on a quadrant chart; channel width; DFS state machine; TPCv1 with
  a worked `Tx_Ideal`; spatial reuse and the constant-product rule; admission control (ADDTS/TSPEC,
  SCS and QoS Characteristics); steering actuators and the MBO vocabulary; a numeric steering score
  example where the weakest-signal AP wins by 24 points; the six-phase roam with sequence and state
  diagrams and a time-budget bar chart; the handoff comparison table; EasyMesh vs MAPC; what AI
  changes; tuning and a symptom-cause table; an interview package.

### Numbers now anchored (all sourced)
- Cisco: off-channel <= 70 ms, 0.2% of airtime, defers if critical traffic was seen in the last 100 ms;
  RF neighbourhood forms at -80 dBm; DCA interval 600 s; DCA sensitivity High 5 dB / Medium 10 dB
  (2.4 GHz) and 15 dB (5 GHz) / Low 20 dB (8.10 config guide; older releases list Low 30/35 dB).
- Cisco TPCv1: `Tx_Ideal = Tx_Max + (TPC_threshold - RSSI_3rd)`; threshold default -70 dBm (was -65
  before 4.1.85.0, range -50 to -80); third-loudest neighbour because there are three non-overlapping
  2.4 GHz channels; hysteresis 3 dB up / 6 dB down; TPC only turns power down; needs >= 4 APs.
  Coverage hole defaults: min client level 3, data/voice RSSI -80 dBm, exception level 25%.
- Aruba: ARM distributed (AOS 6 / Instant), AirMatch centralised on the Mobility Conductor from 24 h
  of data deployed once a day plus event-driven changes, ClientMatch for per-client sticky/band/load.
- Ruckus ChannelFly: capacity not interference, assess every 15 s, react to a large drop in <= 15 s,
  802.11h CSA, 30-60 min to settle; ChannelFly 2.0 = 20 s background scan, RSSI-weighted neighbour RX
  utilisation, ~16-entry table, CCF default 33, Full Optimization Period.
- Mist: two-tier (global daily 2-3 AM local + event-driven local), reinforcement learning validated
  against the Capacity SLE, only power range and channel list exposed, cross-site learning.
- Spatial reuse: OBSS_PDmin -82, OBSS_PDmax -62, `TX_PWRmax = TX_PWRref - (OBSS_PD - OBSS_PDmin)`;
  BSS colour 6 bits, collision probability 1/63. DFS: CAC 60 s (600 s on 120/124/128), CMT 10 s, NOP
  30 min.
