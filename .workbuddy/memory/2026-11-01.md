## WLAN — new note 02-16, radio resource management

### What was written
- `knowledge-graph/WLAN/02-16 WLAN - Radio Resource Management - Channels, Power, Admission and the
  Roaming Algorithms.md` — 1286 lines, 15 Mermaid diagrams, 27 tables, 30 references, English only.
- Thesis: IEEE 802.11 defines measurements (11k), requests (11v), fast security (11r) and etiquette
  (11h) but never says who assigns a channel or when a STA must move. RRM is a vendor function built
  on standardised primitives, and every argument reduces to: what can it measure, what may it force,

  the loop plus the authority-gradient table (six of ten actuators are refusable); the measurement

  a worked `Tx_Ideal`; spatial reuse and the constant-product rule; admission control (ADDTS/TSPEC,

  example where the weakest-signal AP wins by 24 points; the six-phase roam with sequence and state
  diagrams and a time-budget bar chart; the handoff comparison table; EasyMesh vs MAPC; what AI
  changes; tuning and a symptom-cause table; an interview package.

### Numbers now anchored (all sourced)

### Fact corrections (important — 02-16 was wrong and has been fixed)
- **The 600 s CAC is ETSI, not FCC.** EN 301 893 Annex D Table D.1 note 1: 10 minutes for any channel
  whose nominal bandwidth falls completely or partly in 5600-5650 MHz. The current eCFR text of
  47 CFR 15.407(h)(2) has a flat **60 s** CAC and no 5600-5650 exception; the US answer to TDWR is the
  FCC 14-30 waveform test (15 PRI values from a list of 23, plus 15 random in 518-3066 µs).
- **Uniform spreading is not an FCC requirement any more** (removed in the 2014 revision, which also
  permitted manual channel selection); ETSI still requires it, masters only.
- **CCTT differs**: 200 ms + aggregate 60 ms (FCC KDB 905462 D02) vs **1 s** (ETSI).
- 02-16 §3.6 diagram, table, DFS anchor and a new pointer paragraph all corrected; 02-17 added to its
  Related list first.

### Numbers now anchored (all sourced)
- FCC: threshold -64 dBm (EIRP 200 mW-1 W) / -62 dBm (<200 mW and PSD <10 dBm/MHz), averaged over 1 µs
  referenced to 0 dBi; sense at 100 % of emission bandwidth; CAC 60 s; CMT 10 s with <=200 ms of normal
  traffic plus intermittent control; NOP >=30 min from detection; TPC >=6 dB below a mean EIRP of
  30 dBm, not required below 500 mW; 15.407(i) forbids the operator disabling DFS.
- FCC test procedure: Bin 0 = 1 µs / 1428 µs PRI / 18 pulses; types 1-4 at 60 % each with an 80 %
